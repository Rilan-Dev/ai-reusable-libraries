# Prompt 10 — End-of-session handoff

Before ending:
1. Record changes made and paths touched.
2. Append a chronological WORKLOG entry with actions, commits, evidence, failures and outcomes.
3. Update PROJECT_STATE, CURRENT_PHASE, NEXT_ACTION, CHAT_CONTINUITY and VERIFICATION_STATE.
4. Update ISSUES/RISKS/DECISIONS only when warranted; preserve history.
5. Record missing features/candidates, unresolved dependencies, and evidence still awaited, including exactly how to obtain it.
6. State the next single actionable task and prerequisites.
7. Tell the next agent what not to repeat, what not to modify, and which branch/ref is authoritative.
8. Do not imply tests, builds, Actions, deployment or verification succeeded unless their results were actually observed.
