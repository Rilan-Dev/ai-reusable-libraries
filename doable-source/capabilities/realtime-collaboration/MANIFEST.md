# Realtime AI Collaboration

Immutable Doable source: `doable-source/services/ws/src/` plus `doable-source/apps/web/src/modules/collaboration/`.

Includes WebSocket rooms, Yjs/CRDT synchronization, presence/cursors, team chat, AI chat synchronization, AI stream/tool events, collaborative preview/file-tab state, and visual-edit collaboration.

Host boundaries: identity, authorization, room persistence, websocket origin/auth, database, deployment topology.
